Frequently asked questions

Key details about the platform, supported instruments, MT5 connection and account funding.

What is the minimum starting deposit?

The stated starting trading-account capital is USD 200. This amount stays in your broker account. It is not a fee paid to VORTEXT AI. Your broker may require a different minimum or impose instrument-specific margin requirements.

Does VORTEXT AI guarantee returns?

No. Automated trading carries significant risk. Backtests, demo results and prior performance do not guarantee future results. You can lose some or all of the capital in a live account.

Does the website place or manage trades?

No. The VORTEXT AI website dashboard is read-only. The separate MT5 bot may execute trades according to its deployed configuration; the website itself cannot place, change or close orders.

Do I need MetaTrader 5?

Yes. The trading system connects to a MetaTrader 5 terminal and a compatible broker account. Exact symbol names, suffixes, contract specifications and available market data vary by broker.

Which instruments are currently configured?

The current configured roster contains XAUUSD, EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, NAS100, US30 and BTCUSD. Instrument availability depends on the broker and enabled bot configuration. XAGUSD is not included in the currently released roster.

Is the multi-account dashboard available now?

Not yet. The multi-account publisher and dashboard integration must be pushed, reviewed, deployed and validated before multi-account data can be presented to customers. Until then, do not treat the website as a live multi-account service.

Why might the dashboard show no trades?

The dashboard requires a connected MT5 publisher, server-side feed credentials, an authorized signed-in account and a recent verified snapshot. No sample trades are shown as live data.

Should I share my MT5 password with support?

No. Never send your broker password, one-time code, private API key, HMAC secret or full account credentials. Support may ask for a redacted screenshot, broker/server name and the approximate time of an issue.
