Privacy

Privacy notice

Last updated: October 31, 2026. This notice explains the main categories of information used by the VORTEX AI website and private dashboard.

1. Information processed

  • Account information: email address and authentication/session information supplied through the configured sign-in provider. The browser stores the active Supabase session so the dashboard can maintain sign-in state; sign out on shared devices.
  • Trading telemetry: account-level MT5 snapshot fields such as broker/server name, account currency, balance/equity, open positions and bot-tagged deal history when the publisher is connected.
  • Technical information: basic request, security and diagnostic logs processed by the website hosting and authentication infrastructure.
  • Support information: details you choose to send through the published support channel.

2. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, enforce dashboard access permissions, display authorized trading snapshots, diagnose service issues and respond to support requests.

3. Service providers

The website uses Vercel for hosting, Supabase for authentication and the trade-snapshot database, and jsDelivr to deliver the pinned Supabase JavaScript client (version 2.118.0). When the browser downloads that client, the CDN may receive standard connection metadata such as IP address, user agent and request details. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy documentation.

4. Access controls and security

The trade dashboard is intended to require a valid sign-in and server-side authorization. Privileged database credentials and feed-signing secrets must remain server-side and must never be placed in browser code or shared through support messages. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

5. Retention

No fixed retention periods have been approved or published for authentication data, trading snapshots, support enquiries or diagnostic logs. The retention schedule must be finalized and disclosed before customer onboarding. Until then, VORTEX AI is operating in a limited pre-launch state and should not be used to submit identity documents or other unnecessary sensitive information.

6. Your choices

You should use the sign-out control on shared devices and keep your sign-in credentials secure. A verified privacy contact route has not yet been published. The operator must provide a monitored contact method and a process for access, correction and deletion requests before customer onboarding.

7. Children

VORTEX AI is not intended for children. Do not use the service if you are not legally permitted to enter into the relevant agreements in your jurisdiction.

8. Changes and contact

This notice may change as the product, infrastructure and legal requirements evolve. The operator's full legal identity, operating jurisdiction, privacy contact and finalized retention schedule remain unresolved. Do not treat this draft as the completed privacy notice.

PRE-LAUNCH NOTICE: this is a preliminary privacy disclosure, not a completed legal notice. Public customer onboarding must remain closed until the operator identity, jurisdiction, privacy contact, retention schedule and applicable legal obligations are confirmed and reviewed.
