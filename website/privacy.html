Privacy

Privacy notice

Last updated: October 31, 2026. This notice explains the main categories of information used by the VORTEXT AI website and private dashboard.

1. Information processed

  • Account information: email address and authentication/session information supplied through the configured sign-in provider.
  • Trading telemetry: account-level MT5 snapshot fields such as broker/server name, account currency, balance/equity, open positions and bot-tagged deal history when the publisher is connected.
  • Technical information: basic request, security and diagnostic logs processed by the website hosting and authentication infrastructure.
  • Support information: details you choose to send through the published support channel.

2. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, enforce dashboard access permissions, display authorized trading snapshots, diagnose service issues and respond to support requests.

3. Service providers

The website uses Vercel for hosting and Supabase for authentication and the trade-snapshot database. These providers process information to deliver the service under their own terms and privacy documentation.

4. Access controls and security

The trade dashboard is intended to require a valid sign-in and server-side authorization. Privileged database credentials and feed-signing secrets must remain server-side and must never be placed in browser code or shared through support messages. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

5. Retention

Information is retained only for as long as needed for authentication, dashboard operation, security, troubleshooting and applicable recordkeeping. A precise retention schedule has not yet been published; this notice should be updated when operational retention settings are finalized.

6. Your choices

You should use the sign-out control on shared devices and keep your sign-in credentials secure. Requests to access, correct or delete information should be sent through the official support channel shown on the Contact page when its profile link is published.

7. Children

VORTEXT AI is not intended for children. Do not use the service if you are not legally permitted to enter into the relevant agreements in your jurisdiction.

8. Changes and contact

This notice may change as the product, infrastructure and legal requirements evolve. The operator's full legal identity and jurisdiction-specific privacy contact process must be added before paid public onboarding.

This is a preliminary website privacy notice, not a substitute for legal review. The business operator's legal name, jurisdiction, formal privacy contact and final retention schedule still need to be confirmed before commercial launch.
