Connect to MT5 on your phone

Use the official MetaTrader 5 app and the broker's own login details. Never send your MT5 password to this website or its support team.

Android

  1. Install MetaTrader 5 from Google Play.
  2. Open account management and choose to add an existing account.
  3. Search for your broker and select the exact server.
  4. Enter the account login and password inside the official app.
  5. Confirm connection status and review Trade and History tabs.

Interface labels may differ by app version and broker.

iPhone

  1. Install MetaTrader 5 from Apple's App Store.
  2. Open Settings or Accounts and add an existing account.
  3. Search for your broker and select the correct server.
  4. Enter your account login and password inside the official app.
  5. Confirm connection status and review open positions and History.

Interface labels may differ by app version and broker.
