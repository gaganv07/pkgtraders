Before you fund an account

  • Keep funds in a trading account opened directly with your chosen broker.
  • Confirm the broker's own minimum deposit, contract specifications, leverage, commissions and withdrawal rules.
  • Start with a demo account while compatibility and feed access are being validated.
  • Do not send trading capital, passwords, one-time codes or API keys to VORTEXT AI.
  • Any VORTEXT AI software or service charge must be disclosed and agreed separately before payment. No checkout or payment collection is enabled on this website.
