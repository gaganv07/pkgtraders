For dashboard issues

Include the sign-in email you used, the page URL, the approximate time of the issue and a screenshot with account identifiers hidden.

For MT5 connection issues

Include broker name, MT5 server name, whether the terminal is connected and the last update time. Do not send broker login details or passwords.

For pricing and onboarding

Ask for written confirmation of the supported plan, any service fee, account eligibility and onboarding requirements before transferring any money.

Safe support checklist

  • Never send your MT5 password, email OTP, Supabase key, bot HMAC secret or recovery code.
  • Redact account numbers, balances you do not want to disclose and personal information from screenshots.
  • VORTEXT AI will not ask you to transfer trading capital to a personal wallet or personal bank account.
  • Use only the official support link published on this page once it is enabled.
